# Seller acquisition UI/UX plan

## Goal and positioning

Turn creator curiosity into a completed seller/store account. The page sells a believable path to revenue without promising guaranteed income. The core emotional sequence is:

**possibility → simplicity → personal fit → concrete estimate → low-risk action**

Primary hero: **درآمد چند صد میلیونی بدون سرمایه اولیه**

Support copy: build products from your ideas, open a ready-to-sell shop, share it with your audience, and grow without buying inventory first.

## Voice

- Casual, energetic Persian written for Gen Z.
- Short sentences, clear verbs, and specific outcomes.
- Money-first, but distinguish revenue from profit and label estimates honestly.
- Avoid fake testimonials, guaranteed earnings, countdowns, or “get rich quick” language.
- Use “تو” consistently.

## Page rhythm

Use many short sections instead of a few exhausting blocks. Alternate white, mint, cream, ink, and coral backgrounds. Every 2–3 sections contains a signup CTA.

## Section guidance and UX hacks

### Header

- Sticky, compact, with calculator and workflow anchors.
- “رایگان شروع کن” is always visible.
- UX hack: login remains secondary so returning users do not compete with the acquisition CTA.

### Hero

- Lead with the revenue possibility, immediately qualify “without upfront inventory investment.”
- Show a visual revenue/dashboard card rather than abstract illustrations.
- Primary CTA: free signup. Secondary CTA: jump to calculator.
- UX hack: three friction removers under CTA—free start, ready payment gateway, personal storefront.

### Money proof strip

- Small cards: zero setup capital, no income ceiling, store ready to sell, payout/reporting.
- UX hack: use operational facts, not unverified platform statistics.

### How it works

Four small steps with large GIF placeholders:
1. رایگان ثبت‌نام می‌کنی
2. طراحی می‌کنی
3. طرحت رو روی محصول می‌ندازی
4. می‌فروشی و به درآمد می‌رسی

Each placeholder includes the recommended future GIF scene and aspect ratio. GIFs should be muted, lazy-loaded, and have a static fallback.

### Mid-page CTA

- Dark or coral full-width strip with one sentence and one CTA.
- UX hack: repeat the CTA after the user understands effort, not only at the top.

### Benefits

Use small cards:
- بدون سرمایه اولیه
- درگاه پرداخت آماده
- سایت شخصی با زیردامنه یا دامنه اختصاصی
- سریع و آسان
- بدون محدودیت درآمد

UX hack: each card answers “so what?” in one short supporting line.

### Who it is for

Use scenario cards for influencers, anyone starting an online shop, brands/companies, and graphic designers.

UX hack: write the first sentence as a recognizable situation (“پیج داری و مخاطبت هر روز می‌پرسه از کجا خریدی؟”) so visitors self-identify quickly.

### Income calculator

Two modes:

- Instagram views: monthly views × conversion rate = estimated sales; estimated sales × average price = gross revenue.
- Sales: monthly sales × average price = gross revenue.

UX hacks:

- Show the output while dragging.
- Use Persian-formatted values and show both rial and toman.
- Label output “فروش کل” rather than profit.
- Keep a visible methodology disclaimer.
- Put signup CTA next to the result when the estimate feels concrete.

### FAQ and final CTA

Answer starting cost, payment gateway, domain, production/shipping, payouts, and ownership.
- UX hack: put risk-reduction questions closest to the final CTA.

## Registration UX

Use a four-step form with progress:

1. Account: identity, phone, email, password.
2. Creator profile: seller type, activity, Instagram/site, audience size, monthly views, sales experience, goals.
3. Store: name, preferred slug, description, category, support details, social identity, visual color.
4. Review and consent.

UX hacks:

- Keep fields grouped by why Chapli needs them.
- Do not request banking, national ID, tax, or verification documents before they are operationally required.
- Preserve entered data while moving between steps.
- Validate required fields before advancing.
- Show the generated storefront URL live.
- Finish by creating the user, seller organization, store, owner membership, and onboarding profile in one transaction.
